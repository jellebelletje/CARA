# CARA question bank

Working record of curated questions. Locked dimensions are final on wording pending the
rubric and launch-condition pass in Phase 2. `pilotCritical` flags are provisional and get
rebalanced across the whole instrument once all six dimensions are locked.

Levels: 0 Not ready, 1 Emerging, 2 Ready, 3 Scalable.

House style for all content: no em-dashes.

---

## 1. Leadership and sponsorship, LOCKED (7 questions)

| id | Question | Pilot-critical |
|---|---|---|
| lead-01 | Is there a named executive sponsor with authority over the pilot cohort's priorities and budget? | yes |
| lead-02 | Can leadership state why Copilot, why now, in terms of a business goal rather than "AI is important"? | yes |
| lead-03 | Do the pilot cohort's line managers visibly use Copilot themselves, in front of their teams? | yes |
| lead-04 | Have leaders said out loud what happens to time that Copilot frees up? | no |
| lead-05 | Have leaders set an accountability standard for AI-assisted output, covering who owns it and what must be checked before it leaves the building? | yes |
| lead-06 | Is there budget for enablement (training, champion time, comms) separate from the licence spend? | no |
| lead-07 | Are IT, security, HR/People and the business function aligned behind this, or is it an IT-initiated project? | yes |

Cut during curation: blocker-resolution forum; committed post-pilot decision point; explicit
out-of-scope boundaries.

## 2. Use cases and value, LOCKED (7 questions)

| id | Question | Pilot-critical |
|---|---|---|
| case-01 | Have you identified specific role-based scenarios for the pilot cohort, or is the plan "give them licences and see what happens"? | yes |
| case-02 | Is there a baseline for the work those scenarios target: how long it takes, how often it happens, what it costs today? | yes |
| case-03 | Have the scenarios been validated with the people who would actually do them, rather than designed by the project team on their behalf? | no |
| case-04 | Do the chosen scenarios rely on data Copilot can actually reach, in M365 rather than a legacy system, a shared drive or a PDF archive? | yes |
| case-05 | Is there a stated value hypothesis per scenario: what improves, for whom, and roughly by how much? | yes |
| case-06 | Is at least one scenario high-frequency, daily or several times a week, for the pilot cohort? | yes |
| case-07 | Does each scenario have a named business owner who cares whether it works? | no |

Cut during curation: prioritisation by frequency times pain; separating Copilot Chat from
agent or Copilot Studio work; drawing on the Microsoft Scenario Library.

## 3. People readiness, LOCKED (7 questions)

| id | Question | Pilot-critical |
|---|---|---|
| ppl-01 | Does the pilot cohort know they are in a pilot, and did they opt in rather than get assigned? | yes |
| ppl-02 | Do you have a read on how the cohort currently feels about AI at work, captured rather than assumed? | yes |
| ppl-03 | Have works council, employee representatives or unions been engaged where consultation is required? | yes |
| ppl-04 | Is there a clear, communicated position on how Copilot usage data will and will not be used, particularly at individual level? | yes |
| ppl-05 | Does the cohort have the slack to learn during the pilot, or are they running at full utilisation? | yes |
| ppl-06 | Is the cohort representative enough that the results will generalise, or is it all volunteers and enthusiasts? | yes |
| ppl-07 | Do people see Copilot as relevant to their actual role, or as something for knowledge workers elsewhere in the building? | no |

Cut during curation: naming specific concerns (folds into ppl-02); recent change fatigue
(facilitator context, generates no distinct condition); a safe route to say "this isn't working"
(owned by Measurement, avoids a cross-dimension duplicate).

## 4. Skills, LOCKED (9 questions)

| id | Question | Pilot-critical |
|---|---|---|
| skill-01 | Can the cohort write a prompt that supplies context, and do they understand why a bare one-line request underperforms? | yes |
| skill-02 | Does the cohort know how to verify Copilot output, and can they say which of their tasks demand verification before the output is used? | yes |
| skill-03 | Do people know what they may and may not put into Copilot, in terms of their own organisation's data classification? | yes |
| skill-04 | Is there capability in the specific applications the scenarios need, or only general Copilot Chat literacy? | no |
| skill-05 | Do people have a sense of when not to reach for Copilot at all? | no |
| skill-06 | Is the underlying M365 hygiene in place that Copilot depends on, such as files in SharePoint or OneDrive rather than local drives, and meetings actually transcribed? | yes |
| skill-07 | Is there a way for the cohort to find and reuse prompts that worked for their colleagues? | no |
| skill-08 | Is there a plan to refresh skills as Copilot capabilities change? | no |
| skill-09 | Has anyone actually measured the cohort's starting skill level, rather than assuming it? | no |

Cut during curation: role-specific versus generic skilling design.

## 5. Enablement ecosystem, LOCKED (7 questions)

| id | Question | Pilot-critical |
|---|---|---|
| enab-01 | Are champions identified by name, and have their managers agreed to the time it will take? | yes |
| enab-02 | Is champion coverage adequate for the cohort, with at least one reachable champion inside each team? | no |
| enab-03 | Is there a communications plan with an actual launch moment, rather than a single announcement email? | yes |
| enab-04 | Is there one obvious place people go for help, prompts and resources? | no |
| enab-05 | Is training actually scheduled with people enrolled, rather than "available on demand"? | yes |
| enab-06 | Is there an onboarding route for people who join the cohort after launch? | no |
| enab-07 | Is there a named person accountable for enablement day to day, distinct from the executive sponsor? | yes |

Cut during curation: service desk triage readiness; peer community space; champions trained
ahead of the cohort.

Resolved: service desk triage readiness and champions trained ahead of the cohort are folded
into the **Ready anchor of enab-07**, since the day-to-day enablement owner is precisely who
would have arranged both. They are therefore assessed without costing a question.

## 6. Measurement, LOCKED (10 questions)

This dimension deliberately does not re-ask the scenario baseline, which case-02 owns. It covers
the measurement system itself.

| id | Question | Pilot-critical |
|---|---|---|
| meas-01 | Have you agreed what "adopted" means numerically, before the pilot starts? | yes |
| meas-02 | Can someone actually access and read the Copilot reporting, with the licences and roles that requires? | yes |
| meas-03 | Is there a review cadence with a decision attached to each review, rather than a report nobody acts on? | no |
| meas-04 | Is there a route for qualitative feedback, including "this is not working for me", that reaches someone who can act on it? | yes |
| meas-05 | Have you decided how you will capture value that telemetry cannot see, such as output quality, confidence or reduced drudgery? | no |
| meas-06 | Is there a plan to collect success stories while they are fresh, rather than reconstructing them at the end? | no |
| meas-07 | Is it agreed what would count as a failed pilot, not only what would count as success? | yes |
| meas-08 | Have you decided the reporting granularity and the privacy line, for example group level rather than individual level? | yes |
| meas-09 | Is the pilot long enough for a habit to form before you judge it? | yes |
| meas-10 | Will you re-measure the people-side baseline at the end, using the same instrument you used at the start? | no |

Nothing cut. All ten candidates kept.

Deliberate pairings to preserve in the rubric work: meas-08 (the privacy decision) with ppl-04
(communicating that decision), and meas-10 (the closing measurement) with ppl-02 (the opening
one).
